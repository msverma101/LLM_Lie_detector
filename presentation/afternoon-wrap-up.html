03 · five approaches on the table

We had five ideas. We ran one.

A

Liar auditor

Two identical small models, one fine-tuned to lie. Claude gets weights, probe scores, or both. Who's the liar?

📝 proposed
B

The ladder

Detect → trace probe direction into MLPs/heads → edit it out → audit without evals.

📝 proposed
C

Claude research loop

Claude rewrites the probe code; a fixed harness scores it on unseen lie families; keep or revert.

✅ ran today
D

Brute-force control

Random hyperparameter search. Same budget, same keep rule. Does the LLM beat dice?

🔧 built, not run
E

WSP on the probe

LLM edits the trained probe weights directly (θ→θ+Δθ), vs. gradient fine-tuning. Fred's protocol.

📝 protocol ready

The middle of the network 🎯

Layer 13 ≈ 65% · layers 10–16 ≈ 97% of the weight · Qwen3.5-2B has 24 layers

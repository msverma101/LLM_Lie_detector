(1:55–2:20) So here is our idea, and both parts already exist. The new part is the interface between them. FIBS is the lie detector: every time it misses a lie or raises a false alarm, it writes a case file: the conversation, the score per layer, and the probe's weights. WSP is the detective: Claude reads the weights and the activations of the misses, computes where the signal went, and proposes a fix, either to the formula or directly to the weights. The referee tests the fix on lies the detector has never seen. If it helps, it stays. Next round. A lie detector that debugs itself. Experiment one shows that changing the formula works. Experiment two shows that without case files and computation, editing weights is guesswork.

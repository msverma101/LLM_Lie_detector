# Diagnostic decision: retain baseline

No direct repair is justified for adoption by this packet. A localized coordinate-ablation candidate was tested and rejected under its saved criteria. Original detector.npz remains untouched. candidate.npz is an experimental rejected checkpoint, not the selected detector.

The suspicious coordinate 1780 was the largest coefficient in all six probes (2.18–3.27, versus typical absolute coefficients around .017). That supports investigating arithmetic amplification but does not identify a semantic nuisance or establish corruption. Discovery contained 214 rows; group-hash reservation left 43 honest calibration rows (29 groups) and 98 transfer rows (65 groups, 53 positive/45 negative). All are diagnostic development data. An initial broad text read was heavily truncated and attempted the entire packet; context-blind transfer cannot be claimed. Historical overlap with earlier work is unknown. Group IDs are taken as supplied; unrecorded links or translated duplicates may remain.

HYPOTHESIS.json preceded candidate scoring. The fixed candidate removes only W[:,1780]; b/mu/sd, activations and aggregation stay fixed. No optimizer, model edit, training, classifier fit, or internet access was used. Arithmetic per-case logit predictions were checked; these are arithmetic checks, not causal semantic validation. Severe false-positive and false-negative contexts plus correct positive/negative controls are preserved in context_review.json. Near-threshold contexts are preserved separately. Examples span factually corrective Chinese text, denial of known price inflation, fabricated supporting sources, ordinary recipes, and invented business analytics. Labels were not changed; human_verified is false in the packet, and correctness/deception/subjective belief must not be conflated.

At independently calibrated alpha=.10 strict thresholds, baseline transfer recall was 41/53 (77.36%), FPR 9/45 (20.00%), AUROC .8918. Candidate recall was 43/53 (81.13%), FPR 4/45 (8.89%), AUROC .9342. Recall gain was 3.77 percentage points, with joint calibration/evaluation group-bootstrap 95% interval [-9.10,19.45] points (1000 draws). There were 5 gained and 3 lost true detections. Original-family recall fell 16/17 to 14/17 (11.76 points), exceeding the registered .10 regression limit. These separately calibrated thresholds (.47443 and .42379) do not imply matched-FPR superiority; alpha=.10 is a nominal row-calibration rule, not a proven cluster-level FPR guarantee.

Zero-edit, .5/1/1.5 strengths, wrong-direction amplification, six single-probe ablations and one orthogonal norm-matched random patch were checked. Half strength had 44/53 recall and 3/45 false positives; stronger 1.5 removal had 36/53 and 1/45. Wrong-direction amplification gave 35/53 and 7/45; random control gave 38/53 and 6/45. No alternate strength was selected after seeing transfer results. Component effects varied and do not establish a unique semantic mechanism. Correct-control score movement and lost detections falsify a claim that the direction is uniformly harmful. All metrics and transitions are saved.

Thirteen score batches covered 5014 row evaluations (214 baseline discovery, twelve 400-row variant batches), below the 50-batch cap. Bootstrap uses saved scores and does not add detector forward-score batches. audit_edit.py passed with exactly six W entries changed; representation arrays and bias were identical. paired_eval.py compare executed. No skill-validator script is included in the permitted skill directory; an external validator was outside the read scope. Ordinary-fitting controls were omitted because fitting was forbidden. An uncorrupted checkpoint was unavailable, so engineered-fault recovery cannot be established. There was no final calibration/test, fresh inference or natural detector efficacy result.

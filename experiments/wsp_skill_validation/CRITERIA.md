# Evidence status after skill validation

These are the five evidence categories explicitly defined for this skill. They
are not a validated quality score or a reconstruction of every earlier chat's
wording. Completing a procedure does not guarantee a positive scientific result.

| Category | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Software and integrity | Six software tests, skill form validation, eight capture hashes and all 16 raw-activation checkpoint replays passed. Every final prediction matched across environments. | Passed within this implementation. |
| Mechanism and counterexamples | An anonymous evaluator localized the injected six-coefficient anomaly. The natural source-offset hypothesis failed its preservation check despite dose, component and null controls. Initial behavioral-test exposure limitations are disclosed. | Supported for engineered recovery; natural mechanism not established. |
| Prospective selection | The natural primary edit was rejected before final opening; baseline was retained. Favorable final control point estimates were not promoted. | Followed for the fresh natural study. |
| Independent final efficacy | New recorded groups, separate honest calibration, frozen arms and paired group bootstrap. Engineered repair gained 6.74 recall points; selected natural detector gained zero. | Engineered control positive; natural improvement not confirmed. |
| Generality | One model, one editor study and new rows from familiar families. Saved-score replay and imperfect development-agent tests are not independent efficacy replications. | Not established. |

# Five-criterion evidence audit

These are internal evidence judgments, not official workshop/jury scores. Completing
an experiment and confirming an improvement are separate outcomes.

| Criterion | Completed evidence | Remaining scientific limit |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Prospectively committed detector-only question, H1/H2, fixed representation, matched controls and operating ceiling in [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) | Own six-probe ensemble; no equivalence to the official EFC checkpoint or proof of model intent |
| Loop | Ten sequential, independently saved free edit modules/specs; actual diagnostic cases, numerical predictions, feedback, incumbent edits and reversions in [edits](edits/) and complete archive | Some local predictions failed; coordinate semantics and a general error cause remain unestablished |
| Evidence | Frozen candidates, separate calibration, 1,200 final examples, actual false-alarm counts, ordinary controls, conditional random nulls, supplementary ablations and joint bootstrap intervals in [report](REPORT.md) | Primary improvement not supported; familiar families, one study and uncertain residual scenario dependence |
| Honesty | All failures, adverse case changes, the pre-test precision correction, endpoint violations at secondary alpha, and inconclusive intervals retained | No universal efficacy, safety or causal-circuit claim |
| Reproducibility | Complete raw activation/case/checkpoint archive with SHA-256, pinned source/model/data, executed Colab, software guards and activation-to-score replay | All eight capture hashes and activation/score replay of 27 arms also pass locally; independent adaptive-agent replication and independent GPU regeneration remain unverified |

The adaptive execution gap from the first pilot is addressed. That does not turn
this negative/inconclusive efficacy result into a positive one. Replaying saved
edits is not ten independent experiments or an independent adaptive-agent study.
